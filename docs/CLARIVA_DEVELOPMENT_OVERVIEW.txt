PURPOSE
This document presents a public, non-technical account of Clariva's development and the capabilities available in the current website. It is intended for general product understanding and introduction. It is not an implementation manual, technical handover, security guide, deployment guide, or troubleshooting reference.

PRODUCT VISION
Clariva is an educational platform developed by Pro Idea to help university students move from confusion to confidence by bringing academic resources, organised study access and platform support together in one place.

CURRENT WEBSITE EXPERIENCE

1. STUDENT ACCOUNTS AND ACADEMIC PROFILES
Students can create accounts using their personal details, verify their email address, sign in and access account recovery. Their academic profile records their current level and academic timeline. Students can review and confirm their academic information, and the platform supports progression through successive academic levels.

2. ACADEMIC RESOURCE LIBRARY
The student library organises available learning materials by academic level and semester. Students can explore featured resources and the wider resource collection, search by resource title or course, and filter by resource type. Resource records include relevant academic details such as department, course code, course title, level, semester and resource category.

3. DOCUMENT READING AND OFFLINE SAVING
Students can open PDF learning materials in the integrated document reader, move between pages and adjust the reading view. Selected PDFs can be saved to the device for offline reading and revisited from a dedicated Saved Offline Resources area. Saved-resource records are associated with the student's account.

4. STUDENT ACCOUNT PERSONALISATION
The account menu presents the student's account details and academic information and allows a choice of profile avatar. Role-aware navigation provides access to the areas available to the signed-in account.

5. ADMINISTRATIVE RESOURCE MANAGEMENT
The Admin Dashboard provides tools for authorised administrators to upload, review, search, filter, edit and manage learning resources. Files can be selected from device storage or Google Drive. Multiple files can be selected for a batch, with shared academic and resource details reviewed before submission. Administrators can manage resource visibility and featured-resource presentation.

6. ADMINISTRATIVE OVERVIEW
The Admin Dashboard presents account and resource summaries, including account totals, verification status, role and academic-level distribution, resource totals and platform activity indicators. It also provides course-representative appointment tools and announcement publishing.

7. COURSE-REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTMENTS
Authorised administrators can appoint eligible students as course representatives for a defined academic scope and term. Appointments can specify an academic level, department, optional course scope and start/end period. Course representatives continue to use their student accounts while receiving the additional responsibilities attached to their active appointment.

8. OWNER DASHBOARD AND PLATFORM OVERSIGHT
The Owner Dashboard combines the administrative resource-management experience with owner-level oversight. It presents account distribution, verification information, resource-library summaries and platform activity views. Owner tools include user-account management and oversight of administrative access.

9. NOTIFICATIONS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS
Clariva includes an in-app notifications inbox and optional device notifications for users who enable them on their devices. The platform supports administrative alerts for key account and resource events. Administrators and owners can publish platform announcements to all users, selected account roles or students in a chosen academic level.

10. GUIDED TOURS AND CONNECTIVITY SUPPORT
Guided tours introduce users to the main website workspaces and dashboard features. Users can revisit the website tour from the dashboard navigation. Clariva also provides connectivity feedback and an offline continuation experience, alongside access to previously saved offline learning materials.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

- May–June 2026: Clariva emerged as an independent educational platform, with its own identity, public introduction and foundational student experience.
- June–July 2026: Registration, sign-in, account assistance, password recovery and general usability were refined.
- October 2026: Dedicated administrative tools were introduced for managing the educational resource collection.
- November 2026: The student library, document reader, resource submission workflow, administrative and owner dashboards, account management, guided tours, offline PDF saving, academic-profile confirmation, course-representative appointments, notifications and platform announcements were expanded.

CURRENT PRODUCT POSITION — 26 NOVEMBER 2026
Clariva is live at https://proideacodestudio.github.io/Clariva/. The current website provides student, administrator and owner experiences, with course-representative responsibilities assigned within the student account model. The product overview above describes the user-facing capabilities represented in the current website.
