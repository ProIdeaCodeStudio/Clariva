CLARIVA — DEVELOPMENT HISTORY & PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Version: 3.0
Prepared: 25 November 2026
Product: Clariva
Product studio: Pro Idea
Public website: https://proideacodestudio.github.io/Clariva/

PURPOSE
This document provides a public, non-technical account of Clariva's development and product direction. It is intended to communicate the project's evolution and user-facing capabilities. It is not an implementation manual, technical handover, security guide, deployment guide, or troubleshooting reference.

PRODUCT VISION
Clariva is an educational platform created to help learners move from confusion to confidence by making academic resources easier to discover and use. The project began within the wider Pro Idea digital work and developed into a distinct educational product with its own identity and academic direction.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

1. INITIAL CONCEPT AND IDENTITY — MAY 2026
The educational-services idea was separated from the wider creative-services website so that each could have a clearer purpose and audience. Clariva emerged as the independent educational platform, with its own name, identity and academic direction.

2. FOUNDATIONAL STUDENT EXPERIENCE — MAY TO JUNE 2026
The first student-facing experience took shape. Work during this period focused on the public introduction to Clariva, account access, the student journey and the initial academic-resource experience.

3. ACCOUNT EXPERIENCE AND USABILITY — JUNE TO JULY 2026
The platform's account experience was refined through successive improvements to registration, sign-in, account assistance, password recovery, form feedback and general usability. The focus was on making routine actions clearer and more dependable.

4. ADMINISTRATION AND RESOURCE MANAGEMENT — OCTOBER 2026
The administrative experience expanded to support the management of learning materials. The project introduced a dedicated administrative workspace and continued refining the relationship between student access and administrative responsibilities.

5. STUDENT LIBRARY AND PLATFORM INSIGHTS — NOVEMBER 2026
The student resource experience was expanded with semester-led browsing and improved document viewing. Administrative and owner-level overviews were developed to make key platform and library activity easier to understand. Role-aware navigation and account-management experiences were refined alongside these additions.

6. RESOURCE UPLOAD EXPERIENCE — NOVEMBER 2026
The resource-submission experience was improved to support files selected from device storage and Google Drive. Batch selection and review were refined, and the interface now makes clear that files submitted together should share the same academic and resource details.

7. ACCOUNT RELIABILITY AND CONNECTIVITY — NOVEMBER 2026
Further work concentrated on account recovery, administrative feedback, resource visibility, role-aware navigation and website connectivity feedback. The platform continues to receive usability and reliability improvements.

8. OFFLINE READING AND GUIDED ONBOARDING — NOVEMBER 2026
The student experience expanded to include device-local copies of selected PDF resources for offline reading, a dedicated Saved Offline Resources destination and guided tours for the main workspaces. Online account and management actions remain unavailable while offline.

9. SIMPLIFIED DASHBOARD ONBOARDING — NOVEMBER 2026
The guided tours were simplified to focus on locating the main dashboard sections and moving between them. This keeps onboarding brief and easy to follow.

10. SPOTLIGHT GUIDED TOURS — NOVEMBER 2026
The guided tour experience uses a clear spotlight around the item being explained, with the surrounding page softly blurred. Explanations appear in a nearby card, and relevant dashboard navigation opens automatically when a step needs it. First-time onboarding and completed-user replay provide different levels of guidance. The latest spotlight release is deployed. Feedback and verification across additional device and browser combinations remain ongoing.

11. ACADEMIC CONFIRMATION AND COURSE-REPRESENTATIVE COORDINATION — NOVEMBER 2026
Clariva now includes a one-time academic-level confirmation experience for existing students and a course-representative appointment experience. Appointments can be limited to an academic scope and a defined term, while representatives continue to use their normal student accounts.

12. NOTIFICATIONS AND PLATFORM ANNOUNCEMENTS — NOVEMBER 2026
The platform now includes an in-app updates inbox, optional device notifications and automatic administrative alerts for key account and resource events. Administrators and owners can also publish announcements to all users, selected account roles or a chosen academic level.

CURRENT PRODUCT AREAS
- Student accounts and academic resource access.
- A student library with level- and semester-led browsing, document viewing and saved offline PDFs.
- Resource submission and management tools for authorised administrators and owners.
- Owner-level account management, platform insights and activity overviews.
- Scoped course-representative appointments for defined academic responsibilities.
- An in-app updates inbox, optional device notifications and targeted manual announcements.
- Guided onboarding and ongoing usability, accessibility and reliability improvements.

DEVELOPMENT POSITION — 25 NOVEMBER 2026
The academic confirmation, course-representative, notification and announcement release has been merged into the main website branch and successfully deployed to the public website. The corresponding production services are active.

Existing students who have not yet confirmed their registered academic level will be asked to complete the one-time confirmation before continuing to the resource library. New registrations continue through the existing registration experience.

The owner workspace includes the administrative resource-management experience alongside owner-level account management and platform activity views. Course-representative tools are limited to the responsibilities assigned to each appointment.

The latest release has passed the website build and publication workflow. Real-account, cross-role and multi-device acceptance checks for the new features remain outstanding. Device notifications also depend on each user explicitly enabling them in their browser. The project owner should collect feedback before treating the release as fully accepted across all supported devices and browsers.
