PURPOSE
This document presents a public, non-technical account of Clariva's development and the capabilities available in the current website. It is intended for general product understanding and introduction. It is not an implementation manual, technical handover, security guide, deployment guide, or troubleshooting reference.

PRODUCT VISION
Clariva is an educational platform developed by Pro Idea to help university students move from confusion to confidence by bringing academic resources, organised study access and platform support together in one place.

ROLE-BASED ACCESS
Clariva provides separate workspaces and permissions according to the role assigned to each verified account. The role determines which dashboard sections, management tools and responsibilities are available.

1. STUDENTS
Students use the Student Resource Library to browse available academic materials by level and semester, explore featured resources, search by resource title or course, and filter by resource type. Resource records display academic details such as department, course code, course title, level, semester and resource category.

Students can open PDF learning materials in the integrated document reader, navigate between pages and adjust the reading view. Selected PDFs can be saved to the device for offline reading and revisited from a dedicated Saved Offline Resources area. Students can view and confirm academic profile information, including current-level timeline details, manage account access and personalise their profile avatar.

2. COURSE REPRESENTATIVES
Course representatives retain their student account and student learning-resource access. An active appointment grants additional permissions only for the responsibilities assigned to that representative. Depending on the appointment, these permissions can include uploading resources and/or updating resources within a defined academic level, department and optional course scope. Each appointment has a recorded start and end period. Course representatives do not automatically receive the full privileges of an administrator.

3. ADMINISTRATORS
Verified administrators use the Admin Dashboard to review account and resource summaries and platform activity indicators. They can upload, search, filter, edit and manage learning resources, select files from device storage or Google Drive, and submit multiple files after reviewing their academic details.

Administrators can appoint course representatives and define their academic scope, permitted resource actions and appointment period. They can also publish announcements to all users, selected account roles or students in a chosen academic level. Relevant administrative notifications cover key events such as new student registrations and resource uploads or deletions.

4. OWNER
The Owner Dashboard includes the administrative resource-management experience and additional platform-wide oversight. Owner views include account totals, role and verification information, resource-library summaries and platform activity. Owner account tools support searching and overseeing accounts, changing eligible roles and verification status, revoking administrative access and removing eligible accounts. Owner-level controls are reserved for the owner account.

SHARED PLATFORM FEATURES

5. NOTIFICATIONS AND ANNOUNCEMENTS
Signed-in users have an in-app notification inbox for updates relevant to their account role. Device push notifications can be enabled separately on supported browsers and devices. Administrators and owners can publish announcements to all users, selected account roles or students in a chosen academic level.

6. GUIDED TOURS AND CONNECTIVITY SUPPORT
Guided tours introduce users to the website workspaces and dashboard features. Users can revisit the website tour from dashboard navigation. Clariva also provides connectivity feedback and an offline continuation experience, alongside access to previously saved offline learning materials.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY
- May–June 2026: Clariva emerged as an independent educational platform, with its own identity, public introduction and foundational student experience.
- June–July 2026: Registration, sign-in, account assistance, password recovery and general usability were refined.
- October 2026: Dedicated administrative tools were introduced for managing the educational resource collection.
- November 2026: The student library, document reader, resource submission workflow, administrative and owner dashboards, account management, guided tours, offline PDF saving, academic-profile confirmation, course-representative appointments, notifications and platform announcements were expanded.

CURRENT PRODUCT POSITION — 26 NOVEMBER 2026
Clariva is live at https://proideacodestudio.github.io/Clariva/. The current website provides student, administrator and owner workspaces. Course representatives use student accounts with additional permissions limited to their active appointments. The role-based descriptions above explain the capabilities available to each account type.
