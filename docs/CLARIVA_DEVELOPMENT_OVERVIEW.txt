CLARIVA — DEVELOPMENT HISTORY & PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Version: 2.5
Prepared: 24 November 2026
Product: Clariva
Product studio: Pro Idea
Public website: https://proideacodestudio.github.io/Clariva/

PURPOSE
This document provides a public, non-technical account of Clariva's development and product direction. It is intended to communicate the project's evolution and user-facing capabilities. It is not an implementation manual, technical handover, security guide, deployment guide, or troubleshooting reference.

PRODUCT VISION
Clariva is an educational platform created to help learners move from confusion to confidence by making academic resources easier to discover and use. The project began within the wider Pro Idea digital work and developed into a distinct educational product with its own identity.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

1. INITIAL CONCEPT AND IDENTITY — MAY 2026
The educational-services idea was separated from the wider creative-services website so that each could have a clearer purpose and audience. Clariva emerged as the independent educational platform, with its own name, identity and academic direction.

2. FOUNDATIONAL STUDENT EXPERIENCE — MAY TO JUNE 2026
The first student-facing experience took shape. Work during this period focused on the public introduction to Clariva, account access, the student journey and the initial academic-resource experience.

3. ACCOUNT EXPERIENCE AND USABILITY — JUNE TO JULY 2026
The platform's account experience was refined through successive improvements to registration, sign-in, account assistance, password recovery, form feedback and general usability. The focus was on making routine actions clearer and more dependable.

4. ADMINISTRATION AND RESOURCE MANAGEMENT — OCTOBER 2026
The administrative experience expanded to support the management of learning materials. The project introduced a dedicated administrative workspace and continued refining the relationship between student access and administrative responsibilities.

5. STUDENT LIBRARY AND PLATFORM INSIGHTS — NOVEMBER 2026
The student resource experience was expanded with semester-led browsing and improved document viewing. Administrative and owner-level overviews were developed to make key platform and library activity easier to understand. Role-aware navigation and account-management experiences were refined alongside these additions.

6. RESOURCE UPLOAD EXPERIENCE — NOVEMBER 2026
The resource-submission experience was improved to support files selected from device storage and Google Drive. Batch selection and review were refined, and the interface now makes clear that files submitted together should share the same academic and resource details.

7. ACCOUNT RELIABILITY AND RELEASE PREPARATION — NOVEMBER 2026
Further work concentrated on account recovery, administrative feedback, resource visibility, role-aware navigation and website connectivity feedback. The platform is being prepared for feedback from a small group of early users before wider availability.

8. OFFLINE READING AND GUIDED ONBOARDING — NOVEMBER 2026
A further development stage introduced work on offline access to selected student learning materials, broader academic-resource discovery and guided tours for the main user workspaces. This work is undergoing release and real-device verification and is not represented here as a completed public release.

9. SIMPLIFIED DASHBOARD ONBOARDING — NOVEMBER 2026
The guided tours were subsequently simplified to focus on locating the main dashboard sections and moving between them. Detailed explanations of individual metrics, form fields, filters, PDF controls and routine actions were removed from the tours so that onboarding remains brief and easy to follow.

10. SAVED OFFLINE RESOURCES AND FEATURE ONBOARDING — NOVEMBER 2026
The offline reading experience now includes a dedicated Saved Offline Resources destination for verified Student, Admin and Owner accounts, with a brief feature introduction for existing users. Online account and management functions remain unavailable offline.

11. SPOTLIGHT GUIDED TOURS — NOVEMBER 2026
The guided tour experience is being redesigned around clear, highlighted interface elements, softly blurred surrounding content, nearby explanations that type progressively, and simpler Previous / Next / Skip navigation. First-time onboarding requires each explanation to finish before moving forward; completed users can replay or skip the tour. Deployment and real-device verification are pending.

CURRENT PRODUCT AREAS
- Student accounts and academic resource access.
- A student library for discovering and viewing available learning materials.
- Administrative tools for maintaining the resource collection.
- Platform and library overviews for authorised administrators.
- Ongoing usability, accessibility and release-readiness improvements.

DEVELOPMENT POSITION — 24 NOVEMBER 2026
Since the previous revision, the platform build has advanced to include semester-led undergraduate browsing, an owner-level workspace, role-aware account management, improved dashboard navigation and website-wide connectivity feedback. These developments are recorded as repository milestones; this public overview does not certify that every change is deployed or available to every user.

The role-inclusive, device-local saved-resource experience has been merged and deployed, including a dedicated destination and feature-specific onboarding. Real-device verification remains outstanding.

Structured course-representative coordination remains a future product consideration.
