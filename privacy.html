Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clariva ("Clariva", "we", "us" or "our"), an educational platform operated by Pro Idea, handles information when you visit or use the Clariva website and its features. It applies to the Clariva website at proideacodestudio.github.io/Clariva/.

We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate the platform, provide learning resources, administer accounts and protect the service.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information. When you register, you provide your full name, email address, phone number, password and academic level. Our account system may also maintain an account identifier, verification status, role and profile preferences such as your selected avatar.

Authentication information. Your password is submitted to Supabase Authentication, which provides Clariva's authentication service. Clariva's application code does not store your password as readable text in its own resource database. Supabase processes authentication credentials under its own security and privacy practices.

Learning-resource information. Files and related details uploaded by authorised administrators may include document contents, file names, titles, descriptions, course information, academic level, semester and resource type. These files are stored in Clariva's private Supabase Storage bucket.

Resource-use information. When a supported resource is successfully opened through the student-facing library, Clariva records a resource-view event to maintain usage totals. This can include the resource identifier, the account associated with the event and its timestamp. The administrative dashboard displays aggregate resource-view totals; it is not designed to display an individual student's reading history.

Google Drive import information. If an authorised administrator chooses to connect Google Drive and selects files in the Google Picker, Google provides the permission and file access needed for that selection. Clariva requests the drive.file scope for this integration. Selected supported files are imported into Clariva's private storage for use in the resource library. The integration is not intended to grant Clariva general access to browse an administrator's entire Drive. Google's own services and policies also apply.

Technical information. Clariva and its hosting or infrastructure providers may process basic technical information needed to deliver pages, authenticate users, maintain security, diagnose errors and operate the service, such as browser/device information and server or network logs.

Support communications. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message and any reply details.

2. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate and manage user accounts.
  • Verify account status, roles and academic-level access.
  • Provide the resource library and display authorised learning materials.
  • Import administrator-selected files from Google Drive into Clariva.
  • Maintain resource-view totals and understand which resources are being used.
  • Protect the platform, investigate errors and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service-related information.

3. Service providers and sharing

Clariva uses third-party services to operate. These currently include Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; GitHub Pages for website hosting; Google APIs for the optional Drive Picker integration; and external content-delivery services for certain browser libraries. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose account information to unrelated third parties for their own direct marketing. Information may be disclosed where required by law, to protect users or the platform, or as part of a legitimate service operation.

4. Storage, security and retention

Account and resource information is stored using the platform's configured Supabase services. Resource files are kept in a private storage bucket and access is controlled through application permissions and backend policies. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Clariva, maintain account and resource records, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes and comply with applicable obligations. Some information may remain in protected backups or service-provider logs for a limited period after it is no longer active in the application.

5. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with your account, request correction of inaccurate profile information, or request account deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal or legitimate operational reasons. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

If you use Google Drive import, you can choose which files to select and can decline or revoke Google permissions through your Google Account settings. Revoking permission may prevent future imports; it does not automatically remove files already imported into Clariva.

6. Third-party links and services

Clariva may link to third-party websites or services, including Google and WhatsApp. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider's terms and privacy information before using those services.

7. Children and younger users

Clariva is an educational platform primarily intended for students in tertiary education. If you are under 18, use the service with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required by applicable law. Do not submit information about another person unless you have permission and a valid reason to do so.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Clariva's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page, with its effective date updated. Material changes may also be communicated through the website where appropriate.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Clariva operator, Pro Idea, at Proidea2025@gmail.com.

This document describes Clariva's intended current practices based on the features implemented or being prepared for release. It is not legal advice. The operator should review it for completeness and obtain qualified legal or data-protection advice where needed before relying on it as a formal compliance document.
